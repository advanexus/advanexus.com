    Licensed capacity

    03

    What the license dimensions mean.

    Capacity defines licensed product use. It is not a commitment to build every connection or business flow.

    01

    Tenant

    One isolated organizational and data boundary within the agreed software scope.

    02

    Registered system connection

    One configured source or target connection. Building and adapting the integration is separate.

    03

    Controlled business process or flow

    One agreed flow counted within licensed use. Designing and implementing it is a separate service.

    04

    Top-level controlled execution

    One root execution within the licensed annual volume. Child attempts and retries are not separate public value units.

    05

    Production or non-production environment

    One licensed production or non-production environment. Cloud and infrastructure operation are excluded.

    06

    Evidence and retention profile

    A contracted retention profile for named object classes. It does not promise retention automation, legal hold or signing; storage consumption is separate.

    Pricing questions

    06

    Read the commercial boundary before you compare totals.

    What does the published price include?

    Licensed product capabilities, agreed usage capacity and standard product support for the selected profile. All prices exclude VAT.

    Where are integration and development priced?

    Separately by the integrator. MTera provides integration, adaptation and custom development under its own scope and agreement.

    Is advanexus priced per user?

    The public estimate follows licensed systems, flows, executions, environments and selected software options. User access remains a permission and administration concern.

    Are cloud and external providers included?

    No. Cloud and external provider consumption are excluded. Infrastructure and integrator services are contracted separately.

    Does capacity include building the integrations?

    Capacity defines licensed product use. It is not a commitment to build every connection or business flow. Implementation requires a separate scope.

    Does the regulated profile include HA operations or certification?

    No. It defines software scope for an accepted environment. Infrastructure, HA operation, certification and compliance obligations are separate.

    Is the estimate binding?

    No. It is a software-only starting estimate, excluding VAT. The written license confirms the final scope, support profile and price.
