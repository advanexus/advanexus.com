Use Cases

Which job takes your team too much time?

Different business situations. The same need to connect records, check what happened and put the result to work.

Why does the report not reconcile? →

Compare the prepared report with operational records and the general ledger. Identify the items behind a difference instead of rebuilding the comparison by hand.

What happened to this payment? →

Connect channel, processing, ledger and settlement records. See which stages are confirmed and where a record or explanation is still missing.

What prevents this delivery from closing? →

Bring together transport, warehouse and receipt records. Identify the missing step or document holding up the next action.

Was this request really abandoned? →

Compare application, branch and request records. Separate completed, canceled, open and unresolved journeys before drawing a conclusion.

Who qualifies for this benefit? →

Connect membership, previous benefit use and contact rules. Separate candidates, exclusions and cases needing review, with a reason for each.

Which exception needs attention? →

Compare the recorded event with the applicable rule and approval. Distinguish explained differences from exceptions still requiring investigation.

The file arrived. Is it ready to use? →

Check partner data against the expected structure and quality rules. Prepare usable data for analysis or transfer, and identify what needs correction.

The transfer finished. Was it correct? →

Compare source and destination against the agreed mapping. Review record and value differences before accepting the transferred data.

Frequently asked questions

Before you get started.

Learn more →
What work can I run in advanexus?+

Run supported data transfers, processing workflows and business checks, and build analytics. Available operations depend on your sources, permissions and deployment.

Do we need to replace our existing systems?+

No. advanexus works with supported sources in your existing environment. The evaluation establishes which connections and operations your process needs.

What can AI access and run?+

Supported AI clients can use only the capabilities and information authorized for the user and connection. Connecting an assistant does not give it unrestricted access.

How do we evaluate it with our own process?+

Start with one process and agree on scope and acceptance criteria. Use a pilot when needed to resolve an important uncertainty before implementation.
