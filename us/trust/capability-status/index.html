Capability status

Product truth is stronger when status and boundary appear together.

Public claims use four states so delivered behavior, production acceptance, environment dependencies and planned work are not collapsed into one marketing label.

Explore the platform →

01

Read each capability as one complete public contract.

The ledger pairs reviewed public wording with status, boundary, evidence references, owner and last-reviewed date. Search and module filters help locate a capability; they never turn a conditional or planned item into an available one.

Scope

What the current product contract actually supports.

Boundary

The permission, connector, deployment or evidence condition that must remain visible with the claim.

Evidence

Repository documentation or test references used for review, not a substitute for target-environment acceptance.

Review date and owner

Who must revisit the claim when product or public wording changes.

02

Available

The capability exists in the current product contract with supporting implementation or test evidence. Connector, permission and bounded operation limits can still apply.

03

Production-oriented

The capability exists, but infrastructure, authorization, storage, recovery, browser or live-service acceptance is required for the exact production deployment.

04

Environment-dependent

Availability depends on driver, vendor runtime, network, secrets, worker, storage, model provider or deployment policy.

05

Planned

The capability is a documented hardening or future phase. It is not presented as something a current user can rely on.

06

Last reviewed is not a permanent certification.

Claim records link public wording to evidence references and review date. A product change requires claim and translation review before the public build is released.

07

Status never erases the exact deployment gate.

Production sign-off belongs to an exact source revision, immutable images, target infrastructure and recorded migration, authorization, recovery, storage, connector, browser and operational acceptance. Repository tests, a readiness label or this public ledger cannot certify a customer environment by themselves.

In brief

Do all connectors support the same operations?

No. Source, driver and deployment contracts decide metadata, preview, query, transfer, write, quality and analytics behavior for the exact connector environment.

Does a deployment become production-ready automatically?

No. Production sign-off requires the exact revision, immutable images, target infrastructure and recorded migration, authorization, recovery, storage, connector and operational acceptance.
