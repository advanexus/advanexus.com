advanexus Assurance

See what stands behind a result.

Start from a result, execution or recorded event. Follow its available versions and relationships, inspect supporting evidence and see what remains unverified before deciding the next step.

01

Investigate without replacing the original records.

The original modules remain responsible for their data and actions. Assurance brings supported events, relationships and saved snapshots into a separate investigation view. That view can be rebuilt without deleting the findings and cases managed in Assurance.

02

See permitted projects together, without widening access.

Your organization has one shared investigation view, filtered to the projects your current membership allows. Choose a time period and drill into results. When a signal uses different project, environment or module filters, that difference remains visible.

01Trends and distributions by outcome and module

02Recorded contributions linked to their supporting events

03View health, completeness and freshness

04Clear loading, empty, restricted, stale, shortened and error states

03

Find the exact event behind the question.

Search recorded evidence by person, project, module, object, action, outcome, execution, connection, integrity, completeness or time. Browse results page by page, or open an exact record directly; it need not appear among the first results.

01Table, timeline, grouped and size-limited graph views

02Private saved views that retain the opener's current access limits

03Links back to the responsible module, checked against current permissions

04

Open the detail behind an object or a person's activity.

Entity 360 combines an allowed object's owner, versions, timeline, relationships, runs, findings, cases and repeatability checks. User 360 connects recorded activity by module, action, outcome, project, object and day, with links back to the supporting events.

01User 360 is not an employee productivity, quality or HR score.

02Counts remain explainable through the exact events that support them.

03Hidden projects do not leak names, nodes or unauthorized totals.

05

Compare what changed. Keep missing history visible.

Compare two exact versions for recorded changes in metadata, state, schema, settings and checksums. Both values and any display limit remain visible. Missing historical snapshots are not reconstructed from a checksum or today's data.

06

See every recorded attempt, not just a final status.

Follow related, permitted events in time order using their execution, object or source-event references. Retries, parallel attempts, cancellations, partial success and failure remain distinguishable rather than being reduced to one final label.

07

Follow known connections in either direction.

Move from a result towards its recorded inputs or towards later uses, within defined graph-size and depth limits. Each expansion checks access again. The graph shows recorded relationships, not every possible lineage link or the globally shortest path.

QueryExecution → DatasetVersion → AnalyticsRun → Artifact

08

Give a finding an owner and a documented follow-up.

Findings retain the evidence that triggered them. Assign, acknowledge, remediate or resolve them, or record an accepted exception or explained false alarm. Cases add ownership, priority, status, due dates, closure details and checked links without altering the original event.

01Links in a case are checked within the same organization and project.

02A complete editor for comments, tasks and links is not yet available in every browser view.

03Control coverage is a read-only signal, not automatic certification.

09

See what would be needed to reproduce the result.

Inputs and versions, query or code fingerprints, settings, environment, row-level access, output and integrity are checked separately. The result states whether reproduction is complete, partial, unavailable or not assessed, and names the missing requirements.

10

Distinguish verified evidence from incomplete or older records.

Verified, unverified, damaged, pending and older records without integrity data remain different states. A valid checksum of the investigation view cannot verify the underlying business history. A completed integrity check may still include pending or unverified records.

11

Share an evidence package within the approved scope.

Prepare a size-limited evidence package in HTML, PDF, CSV, JSON, NDJSON or ZIP. Its contents stay within your permissions, with an inventory and checksums that allow the stored package to be checked.

Technical export only — not automatically digitally signed, WORM protected, PDF/A or legally conclusive.

  1. Package generation runs in the background with a saved status and defined limits.

  2. Download checks current access, scope, completion, expiry, access token, size and ZIP checksum again.

  3. Stored-package verification checks its inventory, filenames, sizes and checksums.

  4. The preview shows what will actually be included; a report-type label does not silently add another output.

12

Keep sensitive access details out of investigation records.

Credentials, tokens, cookies and connection-login details are removed before evidence is stored, displayed or exported. Access to technical context does not automatically reveal sensitive values. SQL and code are cleaned before their integrity fingerprints are calculated.

13

Make exceptional administrator access specific and accountable.

Administrators see sanitized summaries across organizations. Opening an exact organization event requires a recorded reason, a time-limited privileged session and permission for that event. The lookup reads one matching event from one organization within a time limit.

01Details are redacted by default. Revealing a sensitive value for one response needs extra permission and a recorded access event.

02Revoked or expired access, a suspended organization or an identity mismatch stops the lookup, including changes during the read.

03This access does not permit arbitrary SQL, bulk export, unrestricted database-table reads or general file retrieval.

14

Know whether the investigation view is up to date.

New events and reliably queued records update separate organization and administration views. Delivery may repeat an event; its source identity prevents duplicate recording. Rebuild and reconciliation show missing or old records, failures, unknown event types and relationships without a matching object.

advanexus Assurance

15

Answer the business question without reconstructing history by hand.

advanexus Assurance connects permitted outcomes, actors, versions, executions, relationships, findings and artifacts into one tenant-wide operational story.

A decision-maker can see what is known, what remains unverified and where evidence is missing without turning those states into a generic confidence score.

Find the relevant story

Start from a business result, user, execution or exception and follow only the supporting records the current viewer may access.

See proof and gaps separately

Explicit reproducibility criteria and source-aware integrity distinguish available support from missing, pending, legacy or unverified evidence.

Turn review into accountable action

Findings, Cases and permission-checked Evidence Packages keep the owner, follow-up and bounded export connected to the same subject.
