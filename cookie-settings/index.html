04

Why no consent banner appears

A consent banner is meaningful only when an optional technology needs a choice. Showing Accept or Reject controls for an empty inventory would be misleading and would create an unnecessary preference record.

05

What happens if the inventory changes

New optional technology must pass privacy, security and legal review, update this inventory and the Cookie Policy, remain blocked before the required choice and support an equally easy withdrawal path.
