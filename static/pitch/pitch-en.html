Further explanation

Basis of the offer and comparison

The main billing example is a proposed procedure requiring preparation. Existing platform capabilities, published competitor applications and a future pilot outcome are distinct kinds of evidence.

Who it is for, why it matters, what distinguishes it

advanexus connects data work with business findings across existing systems. The initial scope is a recurring problem with accessible records, reviewed rules and an accountable decision owner.

The matrix links competitor scope to named sources. Coordinates indicate categories of focus, not measured differences in quality or speed. Vendor selection requires the same business case, sources, roles, permissions and result criterion. Documented product capability, required preparation and an accepted outcome are separate claims.

Price and comparable scope

Commercial comparison requires the same scope and a 12-month period: software, implementation, infrastructure and AI consumption, support and operational work. A public price for an isolated product is not equivalent to an offer for the complete procedure. No unsupported claim that advanexus is the cheapest is presented.

What is established in the actual process

  • One period and service type: the same cases, active effort across roles, waiting and the same accuracy criterion.
  • Financial acceptance separately confirms the candidate, approved billing, invoiced amount and collected amount.
  • A second authorised user verifies the finding, its basis and its limits without guidance from the author.
  • An agreed failure check confirms the actual effect of a control and safe continuation in a supported path.
  • A second similar procedure shows what was reused, what needed separate preparation and how much maintenance remains.
  • The best available alternative is compared using the same result scope and total cost, not the licence price alone.

SWOT

Strength

A business finding and the procedure behind it

Connecting approved records, prepared processing, controls and results enables business and technical perspectives on the same case. Existing systems remain in use.

Limitation

The billing scenario needs preparation

Reviewed tariffs, service events, acceptance records and financial links are required. The platform foundation does not establish a ready-made invoicing system or proven value for the new scenario.

Opportunity

Work between operations and finance

Reviewing completed services against billing provides a measurable starting point. Shipment readiness and partner-order acceptance follow a related pattern, each with separately defined conditions.

Risk

Existing investment and mature alternatives

A customer may already own a platform capable of supporting the same procedure. Source quality, integration effort, operational change and total cost can outweigh an individual feature.
