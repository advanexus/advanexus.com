02The business problem

The data exists. People still assemble the answer.

Which customer commitment needs attention and which record supports that finding?

01

A status is not a fulfilled commitment

Location and transport status alone do not show whether goods will meet the receiving window, be accepted and have the required documents.

02

The answer is assembled between teams

Transport, warehouse, finance and partners each hold part of the picture. A customer question triggers calls, extracts and checks that everyone means the same commitment.

03

Different problems look the same

A confirmed block, an estimated risk and a missing partner response require different actions. A single red status does not explain the distinction.

04

A new event starts another investigation

After a schedule change or document update, the investigation restarts to establish what is resolved. The earlier finding and evidence of the new state often remain separate.

The cost is not just preparing a report. Every follow-up pulls people from several teams back into the work.
