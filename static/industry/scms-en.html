02The business problem

The data exists. People still assemble the answer.

Which customers, warehouses and overdue receivables need attention — and what supports that assessment?

01

Executives wait for the full picture

Customer, warehouse and receivables reviews are assembled from separate reports. Before deciding, it must be established whether everyone is discussing the same scope.

02

Averages hide exceptions

An aggregate result does not always reveal which customer, warehouse or shipment needs attention. A clear path from the indicator to individual records is needed.

03

Delivery is not collection

Operations tracks deliveries; finance tracks overdue receivables. Without a connected view, missing documents, outstanding collection and resolved exceptions are difficult to distinguish.

04

Every follow-up returns to the team

A director's follow-up triggers fresh data collection even when the underlying check has already been prepared. Teams rebuild material instead of clarifying the finding.

The cost is not just preparing a report. Every follow-up pulls people from several teams back into the work.

Team leaders and operations
