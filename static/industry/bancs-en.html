02The business problem

The data exists. People still assemble the answer.

What needs clarification before the operations review, which team owns it, and where is the evidence?

01

The review trails the question

Leaders need unresolved issues before the review. Instead, teams can end up collecting extracts again and reconciling what each status means.

02

A green channel is not the outcome

A successful payment can still have an unresolved posting or settlement question. Without a connected review, a business conclusion rests on only part of the evidence.

03

The case is separate from the finding

Knowing who should act and by when requires another view. The team, deadline and missing evidence are separate from the finding.

04

A follow-up restarts the work

When a manager asks why, the search for postings and rules starts again. Time goes into assembling the answer rather than resolving the exception.

The cost is not just preparing a report. Every follow-up pulls people from several teams back into the work.
