02The business problem

The data exists. People still assemble the answer.

Is the business review ready, what still needs attention, and where is the evidence?

01

The review trails the question

Leaders need unresolved issues before the review. Instead, teams can end up collecting extracts again and reconciling what each status means.

02

Completed processing is not the outcome

A report can be produced while expected records are missing. Technical success does not confirm that the result meets business acceptance rules.

03

The finding is separate from the next step

Understanding what blocks progress requires another search for the rule, exceptions and preceding execution. A status alone does not show what needs investigation.

04

A follow-up restarts the work

When a manager asks why, the search for source records and rules starts again. Time goes into assembling the answer rather than resolving the exception.

The cost is not just preparing a report. Every follow-up pulls people from several teams back into the work.
